How much does it cost, and how long does it take

A one way private transfer from Cancun International Airport to Tulum starts at $125 USD per vehicle to the town and Aldea Zama, and $145 USD per vehicle to the beach hotel zone. The drive is 119 kilometres down Highway 307 and takes about 1 hour 40 minutes to town, closer to 2 hours to the beach road.

Those prices are for the whole vehicle, not per person. Four passengers pay the same as one.

The drive itself

Highway 307 runs parallel to the coast from Cancun through Playa del Carmen, Akumal and on to Tulum. It is a good road, well maintained and mostly divided, with topes and speed cameras at the town entrances. Traffic near Playa del Carmen in the late afternoon is the usual delay.

Your chauffeur drives directly. No stop at a shop, no detour to a jewellery store, no timeshare presentation. If you want to stop for food or a cenote on the way, tell us when you book and we build it into the trip.

Why not a shuttle, a taxi or the ADO bus

Shared shuttle

Cheaper per person, but it leaves when the van is full and drops at several hotels before yours. Two hours can become three and a half.

Airport taxi

Available at the curb, but the fare to Tulum is often higher than a booked private transfer, and you negotiate it after a long flight with no way to compare.

ADO bus

The budget option, roughly 2 hours to the Tulum bus station, then a taxi to your hotel. It works if you are travelling light, alone and on a tight budget. With luggage, children or a beach hotel, the saving disappears at the second leg.

Private transfer

Fixed price for the vehicle, chauffeur waiting inside the terminal, direct to your door, and someone accountable if the flight is delayed.

Should you fly into Tulum airport instead

Tulum International Airport (TQO) opened in December 2023 and is 25 minutes from Aldea Zama, which removes most of this drive. If TQO has a convenient flight for you, take it. Cancun still has far more frequency and more direct international routes, which is why this remains the busiest route in the region. We serve both, and mixed itineraries into CUN and out of TQO are common. See our Tulum airport transfers page for that side.

Arriving late at night

Plenty of flights land at Cancun after 10pm, which means arriving in Tulum close to midnight. This is exactly when improvising goes wrong: fewer taxis, no shuttle departures and a long dark highway. Our service runs 24 hours and a booked transfer costs the same at 1am as it does at noon.

Frequently asked

Common questions

How much is a taxi or private transfer from Cancun airport to Tulum?

A private transfer starts at $125 USD per vehicle to Tulum town and $145 USD to the beach hotel zone in our Economy tier. Luxury vehicles start at $230 USD. All rates are per vehicle rather than per person and are fixed before you travel.

How long does it take to drive from Cancun airport to Tulum?

About 1 hour 40 minutes to Tulum town and Aldea Zama, and close to 2 hours to hotels on the beach road. The distance is 119 kilometres on Highway 307.

Is it safe to drive from Cancun to Tulum at night?

Highway 307 is a well maintained divided road and we operate it 24 hours a day with licensed chauffeurs and insured vehicles. Late arrivals are common on this route and the rate does not change at night.

What is the cheapest way to get from Cancun airport to Tulum?

The ADO bus is the lowest cost option, roughly two hours to the Tulum bus station, followed by a taxi to your hotel. For two or more travelers with luggage, a private transfer at a fixed price per vehicle usually ends up comparable once the second leg is counted.

Can we stop at a cenote or for food on the way to Tulum?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we build the stop into the trip. Your chauffeur and vehicle stay with you rather than dropping you and leaving.

Do you meet us inside the Cancun airport terminal?

Yes. Our federal permits and airport zone licenses allow our chauffeurs to wait inside the arrivals hall with a name sign, which informal drivers are not permitted to do.
