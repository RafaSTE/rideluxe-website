The short answer

There are five ways to leave Cancun International Airport: an airport taxi, Uber, a shared shuttle, the ADO bus or a pre-booked private transfer. If you travel light on a tight budget, the bus or a shuttle works. If you are a family or a group, landing late, or heading to Playa del Carmen or Tulum, a private transfer is the simplest option and often close in price per person.

Airport taxis at Cancun

Taxis are arranged at the ground transportation counters after customs. The fare is set at the counter for your zone and you queue for a car. It works, but you only learn the price after you land, and nobody is tracking your flight if it is late.

Uber at Cancun airport

Since 2025 Uber can serve Cancun airport through Uber Reserve and Uber Taxi. Price and availability depend on demand at the moment you land, and luggage space depends on the car that accepts the ride. For a family with suitcases, or a 45 minute drive to Playa del Carmen, a fixed price vehicle is easier to plan.

Shared shuttles

Shared vans sell seats per person. They wait until the van is full and stop at several hotels before yours, which can add a lot of time after a long flight. They suit solo travelers who are not in a hurry.

ADO bus and Tren Maya

ADO buses run from the airport terminals to downtown Cancun, Playa del Carmen and Tulum on a fixed schedule. They are the cheapest option, but they stop at bus stations rather than hotels, so you still need a taxi at the other end. The Tren Maya has a Cancun Airport station; check how you connect from your terminal to the station and from the station at your destination to your hotel.

A private transfer with Ride Luxe

Your chauffeur meets you at the Welcome Bar (Terminal 2 domestic, Terminal 4) or Margaritaville (Terminal 2 international, Terminal 3) with a sign showing your name. Before pickup you receive a tracking link with your chauffeur’s photo, the vehicle plate and its live location, and your chauffeur waits up to 60 minutes after landing at no charge.

What to skip at arrivals

After customs you walk through a hall of sales desks. Some offer transport or discounts in exchange for attending a timeshare presentation. With a reservation there is no reason to stop: walk straight through to your meeting point.

Frequently asked

Common questions

Is Uber allowed at Cancun airport?

Since 2025 Uber can serve Cancun airport through Uber Reserve and Uber Taxi. Price and availability depend on demand when you land. A pre-booked private transfer gives you a fixed price per vehicle, a chauffeur waiting at your terminal and 60 minutes of free waiting.

What is the cheapest way from Cancun airport to Tulum?

The ADO bus is the cheapest, but it stops at the Tulum bus station, so you still need a taxi to your hotel. A private Ride Luxe transfer goes door to door from $125 USD per vehicle, which a family or group shares.

Should I pre-book Cancun airport transportation?

Yes, if you want to know the price before you fly, have your flight tracked and find someone waiting for you. It matters most for families, late arrivals and longer drives to Playa del Carmen or Tulum.

Is a taxi or a private transfer cheaper from Cancun airport?

Both are priced per vehicle. The difference is that a pre-booked transfer is fixed before you travel, includes flight tracking and waiting time, and is sized for your group. Ride Luxe Economy to the Hotel Zone starts at $40 USD per vehicle.

Where do I meet my Ride Luxe chauffeur at Cancun airport?

Terminal 2 domestic flights: Welcome Bar. Terminal 2 international flights and Terminal 3: Margaritaville. Terminal 4: Welcome Bar. Your chauffeur holds a sign with your name.
