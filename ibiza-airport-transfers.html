Private transfers from Ibiza airport

Ibiza Airport (IBZ) sits in Sant Josep, about 7 km southwest of Ibiza Town. It handled a record 9.1 million passengers in 2025, and in July and August more than 1.4 million arrive every month, most of them from the UK, Italy, Germany and the Netherlands.

That is exactly when the island runs short of rides. The taxi queue grows with every landing, rideshare cars are scarce, and rental cars are capped in summer. Booking ahead solves it: your driver is waiting in arrivals with your name, the price is fixed in euros and the car is a few steps away.

Ibiza airport pickup

IBZ has a single terminal. Arrivals are on the ground floor, and baggage reclaim opens straight into the arrivals hall. Your driver waits at the exit from baggage reclaim with a sign showing your name, and we follow your flight, so an early or late landing is not a problem.

Know before you land

Taxis at IBZ

There is one taxi rank, and in July and August the queue can be long when several flights land together. A pre-booked driver is already waiting for you.

Uber and VTC

Uber has worked in Ibiza since 2023 through licensed VTC drivers, but cars are scarce at peak times and the rules have changed several times in court.

Summer car limits

From June to September Ibiza caps the number of visiting and rental cars, and popular coves such as Cala Salada control access. Being driven saves the hunt for parking.

The season

The clubs open in late April and close in early October. July and August are the busiest months at the airport, so book your transfers early.

Time and money

Central European Time, and the euro. Tipping is optional: round up, or leave 5 to 10% in restaurants.

Two languages

Catalan and Spanish are both official, so you will see Eivissa for Ibiza Town, Sant Antoni for San Antonio and Santa Eulària for Santa Eulalia.
