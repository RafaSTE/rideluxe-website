About Ride Luxe Mexico

Federally licensed, traveler tested

Ride Luxe Mexico is a federally licensed luxury private transfer service operating across Cancun, Playa del Carmen, Tulum and Los Cabos. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Tulum, Quintana Roo, we have transported over 45,000 passengers with a 5.0-star rating on Tripadvisor across 216 reviews. Winner of the Tripadvisor Travelers’ Choice Award in 2024 and 2025. We hold federal SICT permits, tourist ground transport plates and airport zone licenses, allowing us to meet guests legally inside airport terminals — a distinction from informal drivers and taxi operators. Our fleet includes the Mercedes V-Class, Chevrolet Suburban, Mercedes Sprinter, Toyota Hiace and private sedans, all chauffeur-driven and passenger-insured.

Powered by Serendipity Travel Experiences — concierge, tours and bespoke experiences across Mexico.

Frequently asked

Common questions

How much does a private transfer from Cancun airport to Tulum cost?

A one-way private transfer from Cancun International Airport (CUN) to Tulum starts from $125 USD in our Economy tier, per vehicle. Prices vary by vehicle: Private (Toyota Hiace) from $145 USD, Luxury (Mercedes V-Class or Chevrolet Suburban) from $230 USD, and Premium (Mercedes Sprinter) from $320 USD. All prices are per vehicle, not per person, and include a licensed chauffeur, federal plates and passenger insurance.

Do you pick up inside the Cancun airport terminal?

Yes. Ride Luxe Mexico holds federal SICT permits, tourist ground transport plates, and airport zone licenses. This is what allows our chauffeurs to meet guests legally inside the terminal at arrivals — unlike informal private drivers who are restricted to the parking lot. We monitor your flight in real time and adjust automatically for delays.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Nothing. Your reservation includes real-time flight tracking, so we adjust the pickup time automatically at no extra charge. If your flight is significantly delayed, our team stays in contact through WhatsApp to confirm the new pickup window.

Do you accept credit cards? What currencies?

Yes. We accept all major credit cards through secure Stripe payment processing. Prices are displayed in USD for reference. Final invoicing is issued in Mexican Pesos (MXN) at the exchange rate of the day, in compliance with Mexican consumer protection law.

Do you provide child seats or booster seats?

Yes, upon request at no additional charge. Please indicate the number and type of child seats you need when booking, or contact us through WhatsApp. Available for infant, toddler and booster.

Which routes do you serve?

We operate airport transfers and point-to-point transportation across Cancun (CUN), Playa del Carmen, Tulum (TQO), and Los Cabos (SJD). Popular routes include Cancun airport to Tulum, Cancun airport to Playa del Carmen, and Tulum airport to Tulum hotel zone. Custom routes and multi-day services are available on request.

Can I book a chauffeur by the hour or for a full day?

Yes. Our on-demand hourly and full-day chauffeur service lets you keep the same vehicle and driver for as long as you need. Ideal for beach club transfers, restaurant hopping in Tulum, day trips to Cobá or Chichén Itzá, or a private driver during your stay. Book through the Ride Luxe app or request a quote.
