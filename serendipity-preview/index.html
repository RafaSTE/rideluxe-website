Questions

Before you book

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp. A real person answers.

How much is a private transfer from Cancún airport to Tulum?

From $125 per vehicle in a private sedan, $230 in a Mercedes V-Class and $320 in a Sprinter for up to 14 people. The price is fixed and includes tolls and waiting time.

Should I fly into Tulum or Cancún?

Tulum airport (TQO) is 30 minutes from town; Cancún (CUN) is 1 h 40 min but has more flights. We pick up at both, inside arrivals.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight and adjust the pickup at no cost. You also get 60 free minutes after landing.

Can I book a driver for the whole day?

Yes. Book by the hour or for your whole stay. The same chauffeur and vehicle stay with you.

Do you plan tours and yachts too?

Yes. Our concierge team books private yachts, tours, restaurants and beach clubs, with transport included.
